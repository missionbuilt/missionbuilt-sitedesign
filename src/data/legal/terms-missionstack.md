## 6. The board

The board shows a workout from IronStack on your iPhone on this iPad, Mac or Apple TV. It connects to your iPhone over your own local network, encrypted, after you pair the two with a code. It keeps nothing once the workout ends; your workout is saved on your iPhone.

- **A board is a screen other people can see.** What it shows (your exercise, sets, loads, rest clock and records) is visible to anyone in the room. Put it where you're happy for that to be seen, and close it when you're done.
- **Pair only your own phone**, or a phone whose owner has said yes. Forget a phone you no longer use (the Privacy Policy says how).
- **Use the board on networks you trust.** It works on your local network only, and you're responsible for that network.
- **The board is an aid.** It shows what your phone sends. Check the plates on the bar and your logged sets yourself.

## 8. Training is risky. Your safety is on you.

Strength training can cause injury, including serious injury and, rarely, death. By using MissionStack you accept that risk for yourself.

- **See a doctor** before you start or change a training program, especially if you have a heart, blood-pressure, joint or other medical condition, are pregnant, or haven't trained in a while.
- **Stop** if you feel pain, dizziness, chest pain or shortness of breath, and get medical help.
- **Use safe setups.** Use safety arms, collars and spotters where they make sense, and gear you know how to use.
- **The rest clock, the board and a plan are aids.** Check the plates on the bar yourself.
