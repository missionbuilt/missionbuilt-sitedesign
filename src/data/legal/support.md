## Reporting a bug

Put the app's name in the subject: **IronStack bug** or **MealStack bug**. Then include:

1. **What you did.** The taps, in order. "Opened Today, tapped Comp Squat, logged 3 × 5 at 315, tapped Done."
2. **What you expected**, and **what happened** instead.
3. **The version and build.** It's in Settings in both apps. For example, "0.1.1 (50)".
4. **Your iPhone model and iOS version.** Settings › General › About.
5. **A screenshot or screen recording.** They almost always help. Control Center › Screen Recording.
6. **Whether it happens every time**, or only once.

Please **don't** send your whole training log or meal plan unless asked. If a file is needed, you'll be asked for the smallest piece that shows the problem.

## Your data

### Take it with you

- **IronStack:** Settings › Your data › **Export all my data** writes every workout, your plan and your own exercises into one file in the Mission Built Schema, a documented format. **Export the raw log** gives you the log line for line.
- **MealStack:** plans and stacks go out as `.mealstack` files, in [a documented format](/rack/mealstack/schema), and come back in the same way. History doesn't export yet.

Save the file to Files, iCloud Drive or your computer. You can bring it back into the app on a new phone.

### See what the server holds

- **IronStack:** Settings › Your data › **What the server holds**
- **MealStack:** Settings › Your data › **What the server holds**

Both ask the server live and show you the answer.

### Delete it

- **Everything, everywhere.** IronStack: Settings › Your data › **Delete all IronStack data**. MealStack: Settings › Start over › **Delete all MealStack data**. Each deletes the server's copy first, then everything on the phone, and tells you what it removed.
- **Only your IronStack Social account.** Social › your account › **Delete Social account**. This deletes your handle, spotters, shared cards and props, and disconnects Sign in with Apple. Your training log stays.
- **Workouts and meals in Apple Health.** MealStack removes the entries it wrote when you delete all its data. IronStack's workouts stay in Health. Delete them in the Health app: Browse › Activity › Workouts › Show All Data.
- **Already deleted the app?** Nothing on the server can be matched to you any more. If you had an IronStack Social account, or want to ask anyway, email **support@missionbuilt.io** with the subject **Data request** and, for Social, your handle.

The full detail is on each privacy page: [IronStack](/rack/ironstack/privacy) · [MealStack](/rack/mealstack/privacy).

## IronStack Social: reporting and blocking

- **Report** a handle, a card title or a person in Social. Nobody is told who reported them.
- **Block** a person the same way. They stop seeing your cards and you stop seeing theirs.
- **Email** support@missionbuilt.io with the handle if something needs a person now, or if you think a decision about your own account was wrong.

The rules are in section 9 of the [IronStack Terms](/rack/ironstack/terms).

## Purchases and subscriptions

Nothing is for sale during the beta. When something is, purchases go through Apple. To **cancel** a subscription: Settings › your name › **Subscriptions**. To ask for a **refund**: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). To get a purchase back on a new phone: **Restore Purchases** in the app.

## Stack AI

Stack AI is an AI (Claude, by Anthropic). It's off until you turn it on from its permission screen in the app, and you can turn it off in Settings › Stack AI. It can be wrong. If an answer looks off, email a screenshot of it. Stack AI runs on a monthly allowance per device. When it's used up, the app says when it resets.

**Stack AI isn't medical advice.** For pain, injury, illness, pregnancy or a medical diet, ask a qualified professional.
