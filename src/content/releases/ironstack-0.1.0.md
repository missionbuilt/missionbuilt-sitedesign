### Badges for the training you've already done

- **Bring in your history from Hevy, Strong or another app and it earns the badges your lifting already earned:** plates, totals, tonnage, streaks. Each one is dated the day you did it.
- **The import now ends on a screen called "On the record".** Your sessions, years and total weight count up, then your badges appear year by year, with the biggest ones and any secret badges your history uncovered shown on their own. It finishes on what's next. You can skip it, and it doesn't animate if Reduce Motion is on.
- **"First rep" is saved for your first session logged in Ironstack, so switching apps still gives you that first moment.**
- **Two new badges.** Proven here is for beating a best you brought with you, in a session logged in Ironstack. Paper trail is for bringing in a year or more of history.
- **On Home, badges you earn in the app now come ahead of badges from your history.**
