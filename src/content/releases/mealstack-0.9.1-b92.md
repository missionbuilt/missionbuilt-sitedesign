**Eat it again in one tap.**

**NEW: Have it again.** Three ways to put food you've already eaten back on today's plate. In History, open a meal and tap "Have it again today". On a meal still to eat today, "Same as yesterday" brings back what you had in that spot yesterday. "Repeat this day" does a whole day at once, with one Undo. Nothing is logged until you log it, and a meal you've already logged stays as you logged it.

**NEW: Scan barcode, wherever you add food.** One tap in Stack AI's bar, the food picker and Add a snack. The first time, it asks before it looks anything up.

**FIXED: The add button always shows.** Type a food in the picker and "Add “oat bran”" is right there at the top, even when close matches show.

**Also:** photos and files are under + in Stack AI's bar, beside the camera and Scan barcode.

**Coming soon:** meal times rebuilt around how long your session really runs. The shake after your session lands when you finish, not 30 minutes in, and the rest of the day re-spreads so protein keeps coming.

**Try this:** open yesterday's breakfast in History and tap "Have it again today". Then type a food that isn't in the list and add it.

## Every change in this build

### Today

- **In History, open a day and tap "Have it again today" on a meal to put it on today's plate in the same spot, for today only.** Nothing is logged until you log it. If today's meal there is logged already, or today has no spot for it, the meal says so instead.
- **"Same as yesterday" on a meal still to eat today puts what you logged in that spot yesterday on today's plate.** Hold the meal, swipe it ("Yesterday"), or open it; it shows only when yesterday's meal there was different. Nothing is logged until you log it.
- **Repeat this day, on a day in History, puts that day's meals on today's plates in one tap, with one Undo.** Nothing is logged, and a meal already logged today stays as logged. Before you tap, it says which meals have no spot today.

### Foods

- **Scan barcode is now one tap away in three places: Stack AI's bar, the food picker and Add a snack.** Each opens the same scanner, which asks before it looks anything up the first time.
- **From Stack AI, a scanned food is saved to your foods and "Where does it fit today?" goes in the box for you to send.**
- **On Add a snack, a scan fills in the name and the protein, carbs and fat of the serving its card shows.**
- **In the food picker, once you type, the add button names what you typed (Add “oat bran”), even when close matches show.**

### Stack AI

- **Photos and files are under + in Stack AI's bar, beside the camera and Scan barcode.**
