### Today

- **Today shows when you plan to train, "Planned 6:00 PM", with Move:** 30 min later, 1 hr later, 30 min earlier, pick a time, or back to your usual time. A move is for today only, and today's reminder follows it.
- **When MealStack moves today's session (say pre-workout went in too late), IronStack asks "MealStack moved today's session" with Sync to the new time or Skip.** It never moves on its own, and it asks once. A move made in IronStack is passed to MealStack the same way.
- **Today opens as quickly as the other tabs.** It spent most of a second re-reading your whole history every time it appeared.
- **Tap an exercise's name to open its sets, or fold them away.** Press and hold for everything else it can do, History included. Swipe right on an exercise for History; swipe left is Delete, as before.
- **When you open an exercise, or log a set, the set you are on comes to the middle of the screen:** the ones done above it, the ones to come below.
- **Edit the day: "Edit" at the foot of the day or in the ⋯ menu.** Tick exercises, then Group them, Ungroup them, or Delete them (it asks once, and each one can be undone), and drag to reorder. A superset moves as one block.
- **"+ Prep" on an open card puts a prep set in front of the working sets still to come.** It becomes the lit row, and the working sets move down untouched.
- **An open card always has a Log button:** on the lit planned row, or on the card's foot when nothing is planned past what you've done.
- **When the last planned set of the last planned exercise lands, the app asks once:** "That's the plan. End and log?" Keep going and it never asks again; the End button is always there.
- **The next set on an exercise now copies the set you just logged, set type included.** Log a prep set and the next one opens as a prep set at the same weight; a loaded workout's lines only take over when they actually change something (a heavier line, fewer reps, prep to working).
- **When the plan moves you from prep to working, the working set opens on last time's working weight, or on your last prep set when there is no last time.** Never the empty bar.
- **A new exercise opens on what its name says it is done on:** banded, bodyweight, sandbag, sled and log lifts open on nothing; dumbbells on 20, kettlebells on 35, cables on 30, machines on 50; a safety squat bar on 65 and a trap bar on 60. A plain bar is still 45. The weight stays a dash until you set one.
- **Every logged set now shows the gear it was done in, not just the set that differed, and each set carries that gear in your log.**
- **A workout's targets show on Today before you start and on Up next, with its superset letters (A1, A2) next to each exercise.**
- **Press and hold an exercise in Up next for its Exercise page, without starting it.**
- **"Which day is this?" only asks when a program is loaded.** A workout on its own is just today's session.
- **Loading a workout no longer starts the session.** Load it from Home, Plan or Today and it waits on Today: the workout, its first exercise with the sets the workout asks for (or what you did last time when the workout doesn't say), the exercises after it, and one Start button. Nothing runs and nothing is logged until you tap Start or log your first set.
- **You can change the workout before your first set: "Change" on Today, or beside Up next.** Your program's days come first, then the rest of your workouts, and you can search them.
- **A day with no workout behind it says so and offers a way on: pick a workout, add exercises, or start empty.** It used to open to an empty page.
- **A calmer screen during a session.** Three numbers at the top (time, sets, weight moved); tap them for what's left. "Hold to talk" is a lighter bar until you hold it. "Add an exercise" sits at the foot of the day.
- **Tap anywhere on an exercise's card to log its next set.** The name and › still open the exercise page.
- **The next planned set is lit, with a Log button.** Planned rows show what the set will open on, with your gear, and "from plan" when the plan sets the numbers.
- **A row of squares under the timer shows where you are in the day, one per exercise (a superset is one).** It fills in as you go, and tapping a square jumps to that exercise. The same row is on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island.
- **Swipe left on any exercise, started or under Up next, to Delete it.** It asks first, and says how many logged sets go with it. Skip today is in the press-and-hold menu.
- **Every delete can be undone for a few seconds, and Undo puts the exercise back where it was, with its sets and plan.**
- **Bodyweight sets read "bw" everywhere instead of "0", and on a bodyweight day the header counts reps (or time) instead of "0 lb".**
- **Tap the "new name" tag on an exercise you typed to save it to your exercises, or match it to one in the list.** Matching moves today's sets to it.

### Supersets, circuits and PAP

- **Group… lists every exercise on the day, the ones under Up next included, so a superset can be set up before the second exercise is started.** It listed only the ones already started.
- **In a group, logging a set opens the next exercise's set straight away, on to the last; then the rest runs for the whole group and the first comes back.** The app says "Next" as it moves on. Each exercise keeps its own reps, weight or time; the rest is the group's.
- **Press and hold an exercise to group it with the next one, or tap "Group…" to pick several, the kind (superset, circuit or PAP) and the rest between and after.**
- **A group on Today is its exercises' own cards joined by one rail down the side, under a thin line like "Circuit · Round 1 of 2".** Each exercise has one counter, its own; the rail lights the one up now. There is no separate group card counting the same sets again.
- **"Ungroup" sits on the group's header, and on the group's row when you edit the day, so a group comes apart in one tap.** Undo puts it back.
- **Deleting an exercise with nothing logged is instant, with Undo, and no question.** One with sets logged still asks, and goes the moment you say so.
- **After a set in a group the next exercise comes straight up with no rest.** The rest starts after the round, then A1 comes back. PAP rests 3:00 between the heavy lift and the explosive one, and you can change it.
- **The workout editor in Plan groups exercises the same way, and History labels grouped exercises.**

### How you feel, and notes

- **"How do you feel?" as a session starts: one tap, or "Not now".** It never stops you starting.
- **"How'd it go?" when you finish, with room for a note.** History shows how a session felt.
- **Add a note to any exercise or set from its press-and-hold menu.** A set with a note shows a small note icon.
- **How you felt and your notes stay in your log.** They never go on a shared card.

### Home

- **The welcome's "Log my first set" opens Today, and the welcome and Home say the same line: Log fast.** See progress. A coach in your corner.
- **A lift you logged before under another name (Bench Press (Barbell) is Bench Press) is no longer called a first.**
- **Home is five sections, always in this order:** Today (what's on the docket, with Load it and Pick another), Your people (Support right there), In your corner, Progress (the top insight from your last session, and your next badge), and Ask Coach.
- Home opens with Mission Built, the IronStack name and its line: "Log fast. See progress. A coach in your corner."
- **The name is written IronStack now, the same way MealStack is.** On the Home Screen, in Settings, in Health and on everything you share.

### Coach

- **If Coach takes too long to answer, the question stays on screen with "Send it again", instead of an error that sounded like you were offline.**
- **Coach takes photos and files now.** Attach a photo from your library or the camera, a PDF, or a CSV, text, JSON or .ironstack file to a message: the program your coach sent, the whiteboard at your gym, a page of an old log. Photos are shrunk before they go and aren't kept; the conversation shows a thumbnail and the file's name.
- **Send Coach the program you already have, as a photo, a PDF, a spreadsheet or a program file, and it writes it down for you: every day in order, your exercises, sets, reps and rest, and nothing the program doesn't say.** Weights come from your log. Tap the card to check it on the same preview a program file opens on, then bring it into Plan, as your current program if you like. Coach doesn't make programs up: ask for one without sending anything and it will ask you for the photo or the file.
- **Share a Coach conversation, or the whole transcript, as text from the ••• menu.**
- **A workout or program Coach writes down for you can be shared as an .ironstack file, the same kind the Plan tab opens, so a training partner can bring it in too.**
- **Saving a workout from Coach says where it went ("Week 3 Day 3 saved to Plan"), and the card gives you Load for today and Open workout.** Tap the card later to open it again. The same goes for programs.
- **Coach reads up to 5 photos in one message, as pages of one workout, so a day split across screenshots comes through as one day.**
- **Supersets and circuits (A1/A2) come through as groups, and rep ranges like 8-12 stay ranges.**
- **If a movement is cut off or unreadable, Coach keeps it on the card as a question and asks you what it is, instead of leaving it out.**
- **While it works Coach says what it's doing ("Reading 5 photos…"), and when it takes too long or can't read a photo it says that instead of "no connection".**
- **Coach reads your notes, including today's, before it suggests a weight or a swap.**

### Bringing history in

- **History comes in from any spreadsheet now, not just Hevy, Strong and JuggernautAI.** Pick a CSV from JEFIT, FitNotes, Fitbod, a coach's export or the sheet you've kept by hand, and IronStack shows what it thinks each column is (date, exercise, sets, reps, weight, unit, RPE, notes, time, distance) with a live count of what that reads as. Change anything that's wrong, then check every session on the usual preview. It earns its badges and gets its "On the record" like any other history.
- **Anything that isn't a table, like a PDF, a photo of a paper log or another app's text file, can go to Coach from the same screen.** Coach reads the sessions off it and brings them back to that preview, and nothing goes in your log until you've checked it.

### Plan

- **"Today's session" is the first choice on an exercise's page, above your workouts:** it adds the exercise to the session you're in, or starts today's session with it. The row then reads "Added to today". Swipe right on an exercise in Plan for the same.
- **Workouts hold targets.** Tap an exercise in a workout to set how many sets, the reps, the weight and an RPE, the same for every set or set by set. The row reads "3 × 8 · 135 lb".
- **A new workout opens as soon as it's named, ready for its exercises.**
- **One word for each thing.** A program is weeks of days, each day is a workout, an exercise is one movement, and a session is the time you actually train. The app uses them that way everywhere now, and Plan says so when it's empty.
- **Build a workout from muscles.** In Plan, pick the muscles you want to train (and a kind of training if you like: common, powerlifting, strongman, conditioning or machine) and IronStack puts together four to six exercises from the collection, the ones you've done first, then compound before isolation. Swap, remove, add or reorder any of them, then load it or save it as a workout. It picks the exercises only: no sets, reps or weights, so last time fills in from your log as usual.
- **A program file whose workouts say which day they are comes in as a program, and the program counts the workouts in it, so every day it offers has a workout behind it.**
- **A program you put together by hand becomes your current one when you give it its first workout and no other program is current.**
- **"New exercise" in the + menu: a name, and if you like its category, how it's logged, muscles and equipment.** A search with no match offers it too.
- **"Add as typed" now saves the exercise as yours as well, so it shows up in search and on Plan › Exercises next time.** Yours are marked "yours".
- **Exercise search matches words in any order, ignores punctuation, and copes with plurals and small typos.** Close matches show above "Add as typed".
- **New in the list: Banded Torso Rotation.**
- **Deleting a workout or a program asks first, and Undo brings it back where it was, including in its programs and on Today.**

### Names to check

- **Names to check has a way out for every name.** Swipe one, or press and hold, to keep it as it is or to hide it. Hidden names are one tap away at the bottom of the list, and your sets stay exactly as you logged them.
- **"Unnamed lift" rows offer Rename, so the numbers you said before naming the lift end up on the right lift for today's session.**
- **"Keep as new" saves the exercise as yours, and matching opens on the closest names.**

### Logging a set

- **The bar is one card on the set sheet now.** Along the top, the shape of the load: Loaded (a bar or an implement, plus plates), Fixed (dumbbells, kettlebells, sandbags: a strip of the sizes you own, ×1 or ×2), Stack (a pin; the last one is remembered), Body (your bodyweight, with Added or Assisted weight) or Band (a rated tension on its own). The app guesses the shape from the exercise's name; the gear on the card changes it for that exercise, and the change is remembered, the way its unit is.
- **Loaded: a chip says what you lifted and what it weighs — "Barbell · 45 lb".** Tap it, pick the thing (Barbell, trap bar, safety squat bar, EZ bar, Swiss bar, axle, log, farmer's handles, frame, yoke, sled or Smith machine bar), then its weight from the sizes it usually comes in, or Other… for any weight. A 35 lb bar is just a barbell at 35. The exercise remembers both, and an exercise's name picks the thing for you (a trap bar deadlift opens on the trap bar, a log press on the log). The plates are drawn above the number, one side of the bar, and the line under them reads "45 bar + 80 per side". Tap a plate in the row to put a pair on; tap a plate on the bar to take it off. Farmer's handles count both hands.
- **The bar is drawn like a real one:** the shaft coming in, the collar, the sleeve, the plates on it heaviest first from the collar out (305 is a 55, a 45, a 25 and a 5 a side), the clip, and every plate labelled with its number. The same bar is drawn under the keypad.

### What counts as weight lifted

- **A band counts its rated tension, tagged "banded":** the full rating on a band-only movement, half of it on top of a bar (the coaching convention's average over the rep). Assisted sets count nothing. Estimated one-rep maxes and records never include a band.
- **Log what one dumbbell, stack or loaded bar weighs.** Pounds moved counts a pair of dumbbells or kettlebells twice, a single-arm or single-leg set once, and a set done on each side for both sides. Handles, ropes and straps add nothing.
- Each exercise has a Sides setting (both hands together, a pair, or one side at a time), guessed from its name and changeable on the exercise's page or from the line under the weight when you log a set, which says what the set counts: "60 lb each hand · counts 960 lb".
- **Because of this, pounds moved on past dumbbell and kettlebell sessions goes up, and so can the tonnage badges they count toward.**

### Your data

- **The file format is Mission Built Schema 0.13.** A prep set is written as `prep`; it was `warmup` in the file under a Prep label. Your own log is brought up to date once, the first time this build opens, and nothing it writes or exports says the old word again.
- **Files from before 0.13 are read and translated as they come in.** A file that says 0.13 and still says `warmup` is refused, with the rename named, rather than quietly fixed.
