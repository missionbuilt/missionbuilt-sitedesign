### Stack AI

- **Coach is now called Stack AI: the tab, Ask Stack AI, its cards, the tips, Settings and the badges.** Your conversation, your details and this month's allowance carry over. "Coach" now means a person: bring in your coach's plan the same way as before, and Stack AI reads it for you.
- **Stack AI is off until you turn it on.** One screen says what goes to Anthropic, what never does, and what happens to it, and asks; it comes up by itself the first time anything would be sent, and "Ideas for this meal", "Ask Stack AI for a plan" and "Ask Stack AI about this stack" wait for it too. If you had Stack AI on, you'll see it once. Settings › Stack AI has the switch, what it has cost this month, and that same screen to read again.
