**Three fixes from the watch, the scanner and your day.**

**FIXED: Log it on your watch logs the meal that's up next, and only that.** A meal you didn't log in time, like a late shake, now shows dim, as it does on your iPhone. A tap in the moment the next meal changes is ignored, so it can't log the wrong meal.

**FIXED: Editing a scanned food keeps your changes.** Edit on a scanned food's card, and Add this food when a scan finds nothing, now have Save and Cancel. Your version is kept, and the next scan of that barcode finds it.

**FIXED: The shake after your workout stays after your workout.** The shake or meal your plan has right after your workout ("After the session") is no longer placed before it when you eat the carb surge early or move your workout. When your workout ends too late for your last meal time, the shake still comes before it, as before.

**Try this:** scan a food, tap Edit, change a number and Save, then scan it again. Eat your carb surge early and check your after-workout shake still lands after your workout (on a day your workout ends before your last meal time). On your watch, look at a meal you haven't logged: it shows dim, and Log it is for the one up next.
